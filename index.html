Honest browser notes

  • Voice-follow works best in Chrome (desktop and Android). Chrome sends your audio to Google's speech service to turn it into text, so it needs an internet connection.
  • Safari's speech recognition is limited (Mac, iPhone, iPad — and every iPhone browser, since they all use Safari's engine). It can stop listening or lag. Use fixed-speed scrolling if it struggles.
  • Firefox has no speech recognition. CueCast tells you and scrolls at a fixed speed instead.
  • Keeping the screen awake needs a recent browser (iOS 16.4+, Chrome 84+). CueCast shows whether it worked.

Questions

Where are my scripts stored?

In your browser's local storage, on this device only. There's no cloud sync in this version — if you clear site data or switch devices, the scripts don't come with you. Copy anything important somewhere safe.

Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording happens entirely in your browser and the file is saved straight to your device when you press Download.

How does voice-follow work?

Your browser's speech recognition turns what you say into words. CueCast matches them against the very next words of your script and moves forward only when they match. If it ever loses you, tap the word you're on to re-sync.

How does the license key work?

After you pay through Stripe you receive a license key (or a one-click activation link). Paste it into CueCast and Pro unlocks on that browser. Keys are cryptographically signed, so made-up keys don't work.

Does "120 WPM" really mean 120 words per minute?

Yes. Speed is measured in words of your script, not pixels, so a 120-word script at 120 WPM takes one minute whatever the text size.
